3. SHARED STYLE INGREDIENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
setting and audience: Australian mine sites, FIFO camp rooms, workshops, outback roads and familiar
social settings. The depicted audience is largely adult workers and mates.
illustration: Heavy black ink contours, comic caricature, expressive faces and practical props. Some
pages use relatively detailed cross-hatching; slides 16, 18 and 27 are brighter, simpler poster
cartoons.
colour: Safety yellow and warm cream dominate. Dusty ochre, brown, grey and muted high-vis orange/green
support worksite scenes. Red flags incorrect examples. Pink is used around the nurse topic.
materials: Grubby paper, chipped yellow signs, ripped/taped corners, bolts, hazard-stripe borders and
small hand-drawn warning symbols.
lettering: Large condensed uppercase black headings. Smaller black instructional copy with selective
bold phrases. Source lettering is baked into most images and contains some errors.
characters: Recurring type rather than a reliably locked individual: broad-faced adult workers, often
moustached, in hard hats and high-vis workwear. Other roles include nurse, safety representative,
emergency personnel, pub patrons and family members. Do not silently treat varying source faces as one
fixed character.
humour: Exaggerated consequences, Australian colloquialism, mock official language, mechanical/body
metaphors, overconfident shortcuts, social embarrassment and deadpan warnings.
GENERATOR DEFAULTS:
  - Use the actual topic record and panel scenes, not the series title alone.
  - Generate illustration and exact lettering as separate layers when possible.
  - Use one scene per generation for easier correction, then assemble the page.
  - Keep observed source scenes separate from any proposed rewrite.
  - Keep technical details, procedures, equipment and emergency fields subject to review. This inventory
  has not validated the source advice.
  - Do not copy garbled text, skipped numbering, unsupported specifications or inherited irrelevant
  headings automatically.
  - Do not assume a dangerous action shown as a joke is the behaviour to recommend.
