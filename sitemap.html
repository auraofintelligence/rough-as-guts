Joyful responsible abundance.

The whole place, minus the wandering around.

The aim across these pages is joyful responsible abundance: a laugh that opens a safer, healthier and more considerate way to live. New concepts remain drafts to discuss and review with their intended readers.

Every page

  • Home

    Light satire and useful know-how for a safer shift, a kinder home and more life to enjoy.

  • Men

    A laugh at the familiar excuse, followed by a way to finish the job, share the load and look after the people around you.

  • Women

    From the overloaded handbag to the hopeful dog, the joke opens the door to more care, confidence and everyday ease.

  • Young Crew

    Practical picture guides for building independence, looking after yourself and others, and getting support while you learn.

  • Worksites

    Humorous posters and shared stories that point toward safer work, practical care and speaking up.

  • Original library

    Daz’s original draft deck, preserved with readable transcripts, scene notes and an editorial review of what needs to improve.

  • Systems

    Explore a real situation, show a useful change and keep the person’s dignity in the joke.

  • Product pipeline

    Books, posters, cartoons, skits and club characters with something useful to offer: more care, safer choices and more room to enjoy life together.

    Find the editions, workplace ideas, source review and tools for humour that helps people move forward.
